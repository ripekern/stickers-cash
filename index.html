Questions

FAQ

Why don't you accept cards or PayPal?

Card processors take ~3% and can reverse payments months later — we'd have to bake both costs into every sticker. Bitcoin Cash payments settle permanently in seconds for a fraction of a cent, and anyone can pay, anywhere, no bank account required. So BCH is the only payment we accept.

How exactly do I pay?

At checkout we show an exact BCH amount (rate locked for 15 minutes), a QR code and our address. Open your Bitcoin Cash wallet, scan, confirm the amount, send. That's it — most orders confirm in under a minute.

Which wallet should I use?

Any Bitcoin Cash wallet works: Selene, Paytaca, Badger, Electron Cash, or the Bitcoin.com wallet. New to BCH? Get a wallet and $5 of BCH in about five minutes, then send your first payment here.

What if the exchange rate moves?

Your quote is locked for 15 minutes. If it expires, the amount refreshes to the live rate before you send — always pay the amount shown at the moment you send.

Do you ship everywhere?

Anywhere the postal service delivers: all 50 US states plus 190+ countries. International mail takes 7–21 business days and import duties, if any, are the buyer's responsibility.

Refunds or mistakes?

Overpaid by more than $1 equivalent? We refund the difference in BCH. Sticker damaged or lost in mail? We reprint and reship, free. Cancel within one hour of ordering by emailing orders@stickers.cash.

Can I get custom stickers or wholesale?

Yes — custom cuts, your artwork, and bulk pricing for shops and conferences. Email orders@stickers.cash with what you need.
