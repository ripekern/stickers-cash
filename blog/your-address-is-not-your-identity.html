Your address is not your identity

A BCH address is a destination, not a name badge. Modern wallets give you a fresh one for every payment — here is how that protects you.

Post 15 of 30 · 3 min read · Oct 22, 2026

Your BCH address looks like a random string: bitcoincash:q.... It is safe to share with someone who is paying you. It is a destination you control, not a password and not your identity.

An address is a destination

An address is a lock. The private key is the key that opens it. When someone pays you, they create an output locked to that address. Your wallet can spend it because it controls the matching key.

Modern BCH addresses use the CashAddr format, usually starting with bitcoincash:. The prefix and checksum make it easy to verify a BCH address and avoid confusion with another chain. Older “legacy” addresses still work, and both formats can point to the same key.

One wallet can have many addresses. Modern wallets are hierarchical deterministic wallets: they derive a new address from the seed for each payment. That is a built-in privacy tool, and it costs nothing to use.

Fresh addresses protect your privacy

The BCH ledger is public, and that is a strength: anyone can verify a payment. A fresh address for each invoice keeps your payments separate. Your wallet generates one automatically, so the privacy protection is built into the normal flow.

Your address does not contain your name. It is a lock, not a profile. When you use a new address for each payment, you decide what can be linked together. That is a simple, powerful way to keep your financial life your own.

Privacy is a practice

Fresh addresses, separate spending and savings wallets, and careful sharing keep your activity private. BCH gives you the tools; a good wallet makes them automatic.

Good address habits

  • Use a fresh address for each invoice or payment. Let the wallet generate it. That keeps each payment separate and your history tidy.
  • Keep savings addresses private. Share a spending address for payments; keep the address that holds your long-term balance out of public profiles.
  • Check the prefix and a few characters. CashAddr checksums catch typos, and a quick visual check with the sender adds confidence.
  • Keep spending and savings separate. A phone wallet with a small balance for everyday purchases and a hardware wallet for savings is a clean, resilient setup.

The address is not the person

A BCH address is best understood as a one-time destination, not an account number. You can reuse it, but a fresh address gives you more privacy and a cleaner record. Modern wallets make that the default.

Privacy is not about having something to hide. It is about keeping your financial life yours. A new address costs nothing, and BCH makes it easy.
