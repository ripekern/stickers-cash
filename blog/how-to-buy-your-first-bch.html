How to buy your first BCH

The fastest path from dollars to Bitcoin Cash in your own wallet. No trading, no jargon — just the practical steps to get started today.

Post 21 of 37 · 3 min read · Nov 6, 2026

You do not need to understand charts, candlesticks, or market cycles. You just need three things: a wallet, a way to pay, and five minutes. Here is the whole process.

Step 1: Get a wallet

A wallet is an app that holds your BCH. It does not hold the coins themselves — those live on the blockchain. The wallet holds the keys that let you spend them. Think of it like a physical wallet: the cash is inside, and only you have the zipper.

Three good options, all free:

  • Selene — mobile, clean interface, built for BCH. Best for beginners.
  • Paytaca — mobile, supports CashTokens and smart contracts. Best if you want to explore more.
  • BitBox02 — hardware wallet. Best for larger amounts. You hold a physical device; no one else can touch your coins.

Download the app, create a wallet, and write down your recovery phrase. This is a list of 12 or 24 words. It is the only way to recover your wallet if you lose your phone. Store it somewhere safe — not in a screenshot, not in a cloud note. Paper is fine.

Step 2: Get some BCH

Now you need coins. The easiest path depends on where you live:

MethodBest forHow
On-ramp app Most people Apps like MoonPay or Ramp let you buy BCH with a debit card or bank transfer. Send it to your wallet address.
Exchange Comfortable with crypto Coinbase, Kraken, or Binance all support BCH. Buy, then withdraw to your wallet address.
Peer-to-peer Privacy-focused Meet someone locally or use a P2P platform. Cash in hand, BCH in wallet.
Bitcoin ATM Cash-friendly Find a BCH-supporting ATM near you. Insert cash, scan your wallet QR, done.

You do not need much. $10–$20 is enough to practice with. You are not investing — you are learning. The goal is to have BCH in a wallet you control.

Step 3: Send it to yourself

Whatever method you use, the coins need to end up in your wallet, not on an exchange. Find your receive address in the app (it is a long string starting with bitcoincash: or a QR code), and send the BCH there.

That is it. You now have Bitcoin Cash. Self-custodied. No one can freeze it, reverse it, or take a cut when you spend it.

What to watch out for

Make sure you are sending BCH, not BTC. They have different addresses. If you send BTC to a BCH address (or vice versa), the coins are not lost, but recovering them is annoying. Double-check the network before you confirm.

Start small. Send $10 first. Once it arrives, you know the path works. Then you can send more.

Never share your recovery phrase. Not with a support agent, not with a website, not with anyone. The phrase is your wallet. Anyone who has it has your money.

That is the whole thing

Wallet. Buy. Receive. Done. You just did what most of the world cannot do yet: hold your own internet cash.
