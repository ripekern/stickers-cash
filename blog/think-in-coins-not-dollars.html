Think in coins, not dollars

The price moves; your stack doesn't. How a “satoshi mindset” turns saving into collecting a fixed share of a finite supply.

Post 9 of 30 · 2 min read · Oct 22, 2026

The shift from dollars to coins has a name: moving from a fiat mindset to a satoshi mindset. Focus on the absolute quantity of coins you own, not the fluctuating dollar value, and the daily noise fades.

The land-plot analogy

Imagine you buy 10 acres of land in a small town. Over the next five years, the local government prints enormous amounts of paper currency. The dollar price of land moves with the market. Does the physical size of your land change? No. You still own exactly 10 acres of Earth. Your share of the town's total land is untouched.

Bitcoin Cash works the same way. If you own 10 BCH, you own 1/2,100,000th of the entire 21-million-coin supply. No matter what the Federal Reserve does, no matter how much M2 expands, no matter what the daily market says, those 10 coins are an immutable, un-dilutable share of a finite economic territory.

The variable fiat mindsetThe fixed hard-asset mindset
"I have $5,000 in the bank. I feel safe today, but I know this money will buy fewer groceries and fewer home repairs every year." "I own 10 BCH. I know exactly what I have. Nobody can print more to shrink my share. My wealth is locked in stone until the day I choose to spend it."

How to keep the long view

  1. Focus on accumulation, not price. Don't stare at the daily dollar chart. Look at your wallet balance. The goal is to secure a fixed number of coins — 0.1, 1, or 10 BCH — and let them sit.
  2. Look through the daily quote. The dollar price is an exchange rate, and it changes as the market values each unit. Your coin count does not.
  3. Remember you can spend fractions. If you need $200 for a repair, you don't sell all 10 coins. You spend a small fraction — maybe 0.4 BCH — straight from your wallet, and the rest of your stack stays untouched.

Framed this way, saving becomes what it should have been all along: collecting permanent, un-duplicable digital property.
