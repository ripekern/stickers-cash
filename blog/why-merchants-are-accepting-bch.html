Why merchants are accepting BCH

Near-zero fees, instant settlement, and full control. Bitcoin Cash is a payment rail built for businesses. Here is why merchants choose it.

Post 24 of 37 · 3 min read · Nov 6, 2026

A small shop in the US sells $500 a month in goods. With BCH, they pay less than $0.01 per transaction in network fees. That is nearly free. Over a year, nearly every dollar stays in their pocket.

For a coffee shop doing $5,000 a month, the fees stay near zero. For an online store doing $50,000, same story. The cost stays flat as the business grows.

What BCH gives merchants

FeatureWhat it means
Near-zero feesLess than $0.01 per transaction, no percentage, no per-transaction minimum
Final settlementOnce confirmed, the payment is done — no reversals, no disputes after the fact
Instant accessFunds arrive in seconds to minutes, not business days
No middlemanNo account to freeze, no permission to ask, no company that can shut you down

Final settlement is the real unlock

With BCH, once a payment is confirmed in a block, it is done. The money is the merchant's. No one can reverse it. No one can freeze the account. The funds are there, predictable and final.

That changes how a business plans. Cash flow is predictable. The merchant is in control.

Who is already doing it

The posts ahead show real examples:

  • How a shop takes BCH — the mechanics of accepting BCH at a physical counter.
  • A small business using BCH for international trade — a Vietnamese coffee exporter selling to a US roaster.
  • A local community building BCH commerce — a neighborhood in El Salvador with coffee shops, mechanics, and market vendors all on BCH.

These are not pilot programs. They are working businesses that chose BCH because the math works.

The network effect

Every merchant that accepts BCH makes the network more useful for users. Every user who holds BCH makes it more valuable for merchants to accept. The loop is building. It is slow — money moves slowly — but it is real.

If you run a business, BCH is a payment rail that keeps nearly all of your revenue, settles instantly, and stays under your control. That is the whole pitch.
