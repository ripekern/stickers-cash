Cash, rebuilt for the internet

Bitcoin Cash is what cash looks like when it has to travel through a screen: peer-to-peer, self-custodied, and final in seconds.

Post 3 of 20 · 2 min read · Oct 22, 2026

Bitcoin — specifically Bitcoin Cash (BCH) — is cash rebuilt for the internet so you can get off that ice now.

It works just like handing someone a bill, but online. You hold it yourself in your own wallet, like cash in your pocket. You send it directly peer-to-peer to anyone, anywhere, in seconds. No bank, no Visa, no PayPal in the middle to watch, charge, block, or reverse it.

No ID to create a wallet. Less than a penny in fees, even for $1,000 across the world. Final settlement, not a promise. Money that is actually yours.

People who switch say the same thing: "I'm so thankful I learned this. How did I get by without it?" They sleep better knowing rent money can't be frozen, their donations can't be censored, their small business can't be de-platformed, and their savings aren't melting on someone else's ledger.

Don't wait until you fall through to appreciate solid ground.

Part one is about freedom. But freedom alone rarely changes behavior. What makes the ice urgent is quieter: the steady expansion of the money supply, and what it does to anyone trying to save.
