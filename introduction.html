Why this guide exists

Something's wrong with money, and you can feel it. Prices climb every year. Your savings buy less. Bank accounts come with fees, holds, and frozen balances. Sending money across borders takes days and costs a fortune. Even "safe" savings accounts lose to inflation.

This isn't an accident. The system was built this way — and it's been getting worse. But there's a fix. It's called Bitcoin Cash, and it's already working.
