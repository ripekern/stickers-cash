Stamps.cash: Physical Bitcoin Cash Onboarding

How a simple idea — printable Bitcoin Cash — creates exponential adoption through peer-to-peer onboarding.

What is Stamps.cash?

Stamps.cash lets you preload BCH onto printable cards you can cut out and hand to anyone. Add custom text, images, even set an expiration date. If they don't redeem it, you can reclaim the BCH later. It's the simplest onboarding tool: physical BCH in someone's hand, no wallet setup required, instant proof that this stuff actually works.

The Power of Physical Bitcoin Cash

There's a reason this works: using BCH is what makes it click. You can read every post in this guide, understand the theory, and still feel like it's abstract. But the moment you actually send BCH — watch it broadcast, see it confirm, feel the finality — the abstraction disappears. You realize: this works. This is cash. This is what money should feel like.

That realization is provable: it happens to everyone who actually uses BCH, not just reads about it. Stamps give people that first experience without requiring them to buy BCH, set up a wallet, or understand the technology first.

The Numbers Tell the Story

Handing out stamps without explanation gets about a 2% redemption rate. Sounds low until you realize most crypto onboarding is near zero. But if you take two minutes to talk to a merchant — show them how to scan the QR, walk them through spending it at their own shop — that success rate climbs.

Those are the moments that matter. One merchant who actually uses BCH becomes an advocate for the next ten.

How to Distribute Stamps

  • Door to door. Knock on doors in your neighborhood. Hand someone a stamp, explain what it is, show them how to spend it. This is the highest-conversion method because you're face-to-face.
  • Tip jars. Drop stamps in coffee shop tip jars, restaurant tip cups, anywhere people expect to find money. They're already in the right mindset.
  • Events. Hand them out at meetups, conferences, farmer's markets, anywhere you can talk to people. Even better: run a booth with a simple explanation and a QR code for people to scan right there.
  • Leave them around. Libraries, laundromats, bus stops, park benches. Stick them to bulletin boards. Someone's going to pick one up out of curiosity.
  • With every transaction. If you run a business or sell anything, include a stamp with every order. Your customers get free BCH, and they remember you.
  • Teach a class. Host a workshop at a community center or library. Print stamps, walk people through setting up a wallet, have them send their first transaction. You'll onboard 10-20 people in an hour.

The Exponential Growth Model

The point isn't perfection. It's volume. If you hand out 100 stamps and 2 people redeem them, you've onboarded 2 people who now understand BCH works. Those 2 people might each hand out 100 more. That's how movements start — not with a viral marketing campaign, but with one person showing another person that this stuff actually works.

Here's the math:

  • Generation 1: 100 stamps given → 2 people redeem
  • Generation 2: 2 people give 100 stamps each → 4 people redeem
  • Generation 3: 4 people give 100 stamps each → 8 people redeem
  • Generation 4: 8 people give 100 stamps each → 16 people redeem
  • Generation 10: 1,024 people have now experienced Bitcoin Cash

Just 10 generations from 2% success reaches over 1,000 people. That's the power of peer-to-peer onboarding with a physical, tangible experience.

Why This Works Better Than Digital Onboarding

Digital onboarding requires trust. You're asking someone to download an app, create an account, verify their identity, and buy something they don't understand. Stamps remove all those barriers:

  • No app download required. They can scan the QR code with their phone's camera.
  • No account creation. The BCH is already there, waiting to be claimed.
  • No purchase required. It's free money, a gift from you.
  • Physical and tangible. They can hold it, see it, show it to friends.
  • Time-limited urgency. If you set an expiration date, they have a reason to act now.

The Merchant Onboarding Flywheel

When you hand a stamp to a merchant and show them how to accept it, you're not just onboarding a customer — you're onboarding a business. That merchant can now accept BCH from anyone, not just you. They become part of the network.

Here's how the flywheel works:

  1. You give a merchant a stamp and show them how to redeem it.
  2. They see how easy it is. They start accepting BCH.
  3. They give stamps to their customers, who now have a place to spend BCH.
  4. Those customers give stamps to their friends, who now have another place to spend BCH.
  5. The network grows organically, one merchant at a time.

This is how you build a local economy around Bitcoin Cash. Not by waiting for adoption, but by creating it, one stamp at a time.

Getting Started with Stamps.cash

Visit stamps.cash to create your first batch of stamps. You can:

  • Design custom stamps with your logo, message, or artwork
  • Set the amount of BCH to preload on each stamp
  • Add expiration dates to create urgency
  • Track redemptions and see your impact

Start small. Print 10 stamps. Hand them out this week. See what happens. Then scale up.

The goal isn't to onboard millions overnight. It's to onboard one person at a time, and let them onboard the next person. That's how real movements grow.
