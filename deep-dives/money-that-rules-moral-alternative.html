Money That Rules: A Moral Alternative

Bitcoin Cash is not just a technical innovation. It is a return to biblical principles of honest money — just weights, freedom from usury, and the ability to serve God rather than mammon.

The current financial system is not neutral. It is built on principles that Scripture explicitly condemns: hidden inflation that steals from the poor, usury that enslaves through debt, and centralized control that concentrates power in the hands of the few. Bitcoin Cash offers an alternative — not just technically, but morally.

Just Weights and Honest Measures

Scripture is clear about the importance of honest weights and measures. Proverbs 11:1 declares: "A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight."

The modern fiat system is a false balance. Central banks create money from nothing and distribute it to those closest to the printing press — banks, corporations, and government — while the purchasing power of everyone else's wages and savings is quietly stolen through inflation. This is not accidental. It is a hidden tax that punishes the poor and rewards those who already have wealth.

Bitcoin Cash is a just weight. Its supply is fixed at 21 million coins, transparently verifiable by anyone. No one can print more. No one can dilute your share. The rules are open, the math is public, and the weight is honest. As Proverbs 16:11 says: "A just weight and balance are the LORD'S: all the weights of the bag are his work."

This is what Deuteronomy 25:15 commands: "But thou shalt have a perfect and just weight, a perfect and just measure shalt thou have: that thy days may be lengthened in the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee."

Bitcoin Cash is not perfect — no human creation is. But it is the first form of money in history that aligns with this biblical principle. The supply is fixed. The rules are transparent. The weight is just.

Freedom From Usury

Scripture repeatedly condemns usury — charging interest on loans to those in need. Exodus 22:25 commands: "If thou lend money to any of my people that is poor by thee, thou shalt not be to him as an usurer, neither shalt thou lay upon him usury."

Leviticus 25:36 adds: "Take thou no usury of him, or increase: but fear thy God; that thy brother may live with thee."

Psalms 15:5 describes the righteous person as one who "putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent."

The modern banking system is built on usury. Banks create money by lending it into existence at interest, then charge additional fees for basic services. Credit cards, student loans, mortgages — all extract wealth from those who need it most. The system is designed to keep people in debt, dependent, and working to pay off interest on money that was created from nothing.

Bitcoin Cash offers an alternative. It does not eliminate the need for credit or lending, but it removes the central banking cartel that profits from creating the money supply and charging interest on it. With BCH, you can hold your own savings without them being lent out by a bank that keeps the profit. You can send money directly to someone without a middleman taking a cut. You can transact peer-to-peer, without feeding the usury machine.

This is not about eliminating all interest — Scripture allows for lending to strangers with interest (Deuteronomy 23:20). It is about breaking the systemic usury that enslaves God's people through debt and inflation.

You Cannot Serve God and Mammon

Christ said plainly: "No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon." (Matthew 6:24)

The current financial system demands your service to mammon. To participate in society, you must use bank accounts that track every transaction. You must accept wages that lose value to inflation. You must pay fees to access your own money. You must trust intermediaries who profit from your dependence.

This is not freedom. This is servitude to mammon — the worship of wealth and the systems that create it.

Bitcoin Cash is a way out. It allows you to hold your own wealth, outside the banking system, outside the surveillance state, outside the reach of those who would steal it through inflation or freeze it through censorship. It is money you control, that you can use to support your family, your community, your church, your mission — without asking permission from a bank or government.

This is not about getting rich. It is about freedom to serve God rather than mammon. When you can hold your own money, when you can transact without middlemen, when you can protect your wealth from hidden theft through inflation — you are no longer serving the system. You are free to serve the Kingdom.

A Way Out of Worldly Systems

The financial system is not just inconvenient — it is designed to undermine and destroy God's people through commercial blackmail. When your bank account can be frozen, you cannot donate to causes the system disapproves of. When your wages lose value to inflation, you must work longer hours just to survive. When every transaction is tracked, your financial life is no longer your own.

Bitcoin Cash is a way out. It is a form of money that operates outside the worldly system — peer-to-peer, private, censorship-resistant. It allows you to support your family, your church, your community, without feeding the beast.

This is not about escaping the world. It is about being in the world but not of it. It is about having the freedom to serve God, to support your neighbor, to give generously, without being enslaved by a system that profits from your dependence.

Worship Through Honest Commerce

Every transaction is a moral choice. When you use a system built on lies, you participate in those lies. When you use a system built on usury, you feed the usurers. When you use a system that tracks and controls, you submit to that control.

Bitcoin Cash is a chance to worship through honest commerce. It is a chance to use a just weight, to avoid usury, to serve God rather than mammon. It is not perfect — no human tool is. But it is the first form of money in history that aligns with biblical principles.

This is not about getting rich quick. It is not about speculation or price charts. It is about using money in a way that honors God — honestly, freely, without serving the worldly systems that seek to enslave His people.

As Proverbs 28:8 warns: "He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance, he shall gather it for him that will pity the poor."

The wealth built on usury and unjust gain will not last. But honest money — just weights, transparent rules, freedom from mammon — that is a foundation that lasts.
